Introduction: Bridging Two Worlds

Most people view Artificial Intelligence as a tool just for software engineers, and business management as simply working with spreadsheets. To me, the future is exactly where these two fields connect. At 17, I have realized that the best technology is useless if it does not solve a real business problem. My goal is not just to build AI models, but to create smart, automated business systems. This is why the AI in Business program at the American University of Technology is the perfect path for me.

The Foundation: Real-World Experience

While many of my peers study business only in theory, I have spent the past year working hands-on with enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems. Working with tech companies like INEXBRAND and O Tizim—both residents of IT Park Uzbekistan—I helped implement and configure Odoo ERP setups for actual clients, including projects for local furniture manufacturers. This experience taught me a crucial lesson: modern businesses struggle with data and operational complexity, and traditional software is too slow. They need automated intelligence.

The Catalyst: Driving Efficiency via AI Workflows

To improve my technical skills, I rely on an AI-driven development workflow. I use platforms like Google AI Studio and modern tools like VS Code, Kiro, Cursor, and GitHub to rapidly build and deploy projects via Vercel and Supabase. I also have hands-on experience working with AI orchestration agents like Verdant AI.

My focus on efficiency extends to how I learn and manage information. I organize my study plans, research, and technical roadmaps using tools like Notion, Obsidian, and NotebookLM. Using these tools showed me that the same AI strategies I use for personal productivity can be scaled to improve corporate decision-making and marketing analytics.

Why AUT?

The American University of Technology, powered by Arizona State University, represents the modern, practical approach to education I am looking for. I try to build my projects with a clean, high-tech design, and I see that same focus in AUT’s curriculum. I am seeking a serious environment where I can learn predictive analytics and data-driven strategy. With my math background and practical experience in business automation, I am fully prepared for the academic challenges at your university.

Conclusion: The Vision

Uzbekistan’s digital economy is growing fast, and the IT Park ecosystem shows that our youth can compete globally. By securing a merit-based scholarship at AUT, I intend to bridge the gap between modern AI tools and practical corporate strategy. I am ready to bring my real-world optimization experience and analytical mindset to your campus, proving to be a valuable asset to the AUT community.
